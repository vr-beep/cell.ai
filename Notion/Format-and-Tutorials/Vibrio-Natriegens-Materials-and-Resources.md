# Vibrio Natriegens Materials and Resources

## Overview

*Vibrio natriegens* is the fastest-growing non-pathogenic bacterium known, with a doubling time of **~10 minutes** under optimal conditions — roughly 2-3x faster than *E. coli*. It is a marine gammaproteobacterium, facultative anaerobe, and BSL-1 organism. Its speed makes it an attractive chassis for synthetic biology and rapid culture experiments.

**Lab strain:** Novel Media Strain — modified to over-produce plasmids

**Key growth parameters:**
- Optimal temperature: 30-37C
- pH optimum: 7.0-7.5
- **Requires NaCl** — halophile, typical lab range 0.5-2% NaCl (85-340 mM)
- Doubling time: ~10 min (rich media, 37C) to ~30 min (defined minimal media)

## Media

### Defined Media

A working defined medium for *V. natriegens* typically contains:

| Component | Role | Working concentration |
|---|---|---|
| NaCl | Osmolarity / halophile requirement | 85-340 mM (1-4 uL of 5M in 180 uL) |
| MOPS (pH 7) | Buffering | 20-40 mM |
| K2HPO4 / KH2PO4 | Phosphate source + secondary buffer | 2-10 mM |
| MgSO4 | Essential cofactor | 0.5-2 mM |
| (NH4)2SO4 | Nitrogen source | 5-20 mM |
| Glucose or Glycerol | Carbon source | 0.1-0.5% (w/v) |
| CaCl2 | Trace structural / enzyme cofactor | 0.05-0.2 mM |
| Trace Metals (1000x) | Micronutrients | 1x final |

> **Precipitation warning:** CaCl2 + K2HPO4 (dibasic) or CaCl2 + KH2PO4 precipitate at mM concentrations. MgSO4 + K2HPO4 also precipitates. Keep Ca2+ and phosphate low when combining, or add them sequentially (never premix).

### Sodium is critical

*V. natriegens* will grow poorly or not at all without Na+. This is the single most important salt to include in any formulation.

## Key Papers

- **Eagon (1962)** — Original characterization of *Pseudomonas natriegens* (now *V. natriegens*); established the ~10 min doubling time. *J. Bacteriol.* 83:736-737.
- **Weinstock et al. (2016)** — "*Vibrio natriegens*, a new genomic powerhouse" — established it as a synthetic biology chassis with standard molecular tools. *Nature Methods* 13:849-851.
- **Tschirhart et al. (2019)** — Gene expression toolbox and defined media optimization. *ACS Synthetic Biology* 8(9):2069-2079.
- **Dalia et al. (2017)** — CRISPR-based genome editing tools. *Genetics* 207(4):1469-1480.
